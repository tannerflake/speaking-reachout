# Lead generation prompts beyond universities

Written October 22, 2026. Each prompt below is meant to be pasted into the **Focus** field of a Discovery run. The app already adds Jeff's profile, the sourcing rules, and the JSON format, so the prompts only say *where to look and what to look for*.

## What the data says (196 leads in the CRM)

Jeff's bookings so far are mostly campuses, but the non-campus wins and warm leads point to the same few patterns:

| Signal | Non-university examples | What it suggests |
|---|---|---|
| Booked / happened | Truman Library, Aspen Ideas Festival, World Affairs Council of Atlanta, ACEC (via Leading Authorities, 20 to 25k) | Presidential libraries, ideas festivals, WACs, and trade associations all pay |
| Interested | AICPA & CIMA Tax Conference, NAW Executive Summit, National League of Cities, WAC Dallas/Fort Worth, WAC Pittsburgh, Ferguson Library "Civility in America" series | Professional associations and community lecture series respond to the "what the next Congress means for you" angle and to civility |
| Not interested | Detroit Economic Club, Committee of Seventy summit | Big-city economic clubs and one-off civic summits book far ahead or don't pay |
| Low yield | R Street, Niskanen, other think tanks | They reply but rarely pay. Skip them as paid targets |

**Why this matters for commission:** Jeff's own blasts go almost entirely to schools. Associations, libraries, festivals, and corporate events are mostly untouched, so app-sent outreach there gets the first touch and the commission.

## Tier 1: proven by the data, scale these first

**1. Trade and professional association annual meetings**
> National and state trade and professional associations holding an annual meeting, convention, or leadership summit in spring through fall 2027 with a paid keynote or general-session slot. Prioritize industries where federal policy, tax, trade, or regulation directly affects members: engineering, construction, accounting/CPA, wholesale distribution, manufacturing, insurance, banking, agriculture, energy, and transportation. Look for past keynote speakers who were former members of Congress, governors, or cabinet officials. Target the meetings or education director, not the CEO.

**2. State CPA societies and bar associations**
> State CPA societies, state bar associations, and state hospital or medical associations with an annual conference or legislative/policy summit in 2027 that books a political or policy keynote. Find ones whose recent agendas featured a "Washington outlook," "political landscape," or "election outlook" session.

**3. Presidential libraries, foundations, and civic history museums**
> Presidential libraries and their private foundations (both NARA libraries and independent ones), plus civic history institutions like the National Constitution Center, state history museums, and congressional history centers. Look for named lecture series, forums, award dinners, or member events in 2027 that host former senators, ambassadors, or cabinet officials.

**4. Removed.** This was World Affairs Councils, but an active Tailoring rule says not to reach out to them.

**5. Ideas festivals and civic conferences**
> Ticketed ideas festivals and public-affairs conferences in the U.S. similar to Aspen Ideas: for example Chautauqua Institution's summer weeks, the Texas Tribune Festival, Sun Valley forums, Aspen Security Forum, and regional "big ideas" events run by newspapers or public radio. Find 2027 programs with a politics, democracy, or global affairs track and a programming contact.

**6. Public library and community lecture series**
> Endowed or ticketed community lecture series run by public libraries, library foundations, and "Town Hall" style organizations (for example Town Hall Seattle, Town Hall Los Angeles, Des Moines Town Hall, Boca Raton Speaker Series, Distinguished Speaker Series in Florida and Arizona). Look for 2027 to 2028 seasons that feature former officials or civility themes and sell subscriptions.

## Tier 2: strong fit with Jeff's story, not yet tested

**7. Wealth management and private bank client events**
> Regional wealth management firms, RIAs, family offices, and private banks that host annual client appreciation dinners, economic outlook events, or investor forums featuring political or geopolitical speakers. Focus on firms in Texas, Florida, Colorado, Nevada, and the Southeast. Target the marketing or client events lead.

**8. CEO and board peer groups**
> CEO and executive peer organizations that book outside speakers: YPO and YPO Gold chapters, Chief Executives Organization, Vistage, Young Presidents/WPO chapter events, NACD (National Association of Corporate Directors) chapter programs and summits, and state CEO roundtables. Look for chapter-level speaker events with a program chair.

**9. Chambers of commerce annual dinners**
> State and metro chambers of commerce with an annual meeting, annual dinner, or "State of the Region" event in 2027 that books a national keynote. Prioritize mid-size metros (300k to 2M people) and chambers with past keynotes who were former members of Congress or ambassadors. Skip the Detroit Economic Club.

**10. Faith and public life, especially Latter-day Saint audiences**
> Organizations serving Latter-day Saint and broader faith audiences that host paid speakers on faith, character, and public life: Latter-day Saint business and professional associations, Deseret-affiliated events, interfaith councils, Christian business leader conferences, religious colleges' endowed lectures outside BYU, Baylor, and Calvin, and faith-based leadership summits. Look for 2027 events with a program contact.

**11. Turkey, NATO, and diplomacy audiences**
> U.S. organizations focused on Turkey, the Eastern Mediterranean, NATO, and transatlantic relations that host speakers or galas: Turkish-American business councils and chambers, the American Turkish Council conference, Nordic-American and Swedish/Finnish-American business groups, Atlantic Council-style regional chapters, and diplomatic and foreign-service associations. Find events in 2027.

**12. Agriculture, ranching, water, and public lands**
> State and national agriculture and natural resources associations with annual conventions: Farm Bureau state conventions, cattlemen's and livestock associations, water and irrigation district associations, Western public lands and rural electric cooperative associations, especially in Nevada, Colorado, New Mexico, Idaho, Wyoming, Montana, and Texas. Look for keynotes on trade, immigration, and Washington policy.

**13. State and local government associations**
> Associations of local and state officials with annual conferences: state municipal leagues, state associations of counties, NACo, ICMA (city managers), the National Conference of State Legislatures, and state school board associations. Look for opening or closing general sessions on civility, leadership, or governing across divides. National League of Cities is already in the CRM.

**14. Leadership development programs**
> Paid leadership and civic leadership programs that bring in a marquee speaker each year: "Leadership [City]" and "Leadership [State]" programs, statewide leadership academies, executive education programs at business schools, and corporate leadership retreats. Look for a 2027 class schedule with a guest speaker or capstone slot.

## Tier 3: worth one test run each

**15. Enrichment travel and cruise lecture programs**
> Enrichment travel programs that hire guest lecturers on world affairs and politics: university alumni travel programs, Smithsonian Journeys, luxury cruise lines' enrichment lecture programs, and educational tour operators. Focus on itineraries through Turkey, the Mediterranean, Northern Europe, and Southern Africa in 2027 and 2028.

**16. Affluent retirement and club communities**
> Large retirement and resort communities and private clubs that run paid speaker series for residents or members: The Villages, Sun City communities, Del Webb communities, continuing care retirement communities with lecture endowments, and country or city clubs with speaker programs (for example Commonwealth Club, City Club of Cleveland, Union League). Look for 2027 seasons.

**17. Youth civic leadership programs with funding**
> Funded youth civic and leadership programs that book keynote speakers: American Legion Boys State and Girls State, Junior State of America, Close Up Foundation, HOBY, National Student Leadership Conference, and state Youth in Government programs. Prioritize those with a sponsor or foundation budget for speakers.

**18. Military and national security forums**
> National security forums and military-affiliated associations that host paid speakers or galas: Navy League and AUSA chapter dinners, defense industry association events, Reagan National Defense Forum-style events, and regional national security conferences. Skip the service academies and war colleges, which mostly don't pay.

**19. Africa-focused business and development groups**
> U.S.-based organizations focused on Africa, especially Southern Africa and Namibia, that host speakers or annual events: Corporate Council on Africa, Africa-focused development and business forums, and Namibian and South African diaspora or business groups. (Jeff served his mission in Southern Africa and has a long Namibia connection.)

**20. Bureau partners for more inbound**
> Speaker bureaus and agencies whose rosters include former senators, governors, ambassadors, or cabinet officials, in addition to Leading Authorities: for example Washington Speakers Bureau, Harry Walker Agency, APB, Keppler, BigSpeak, and Greater Talent Network. Return the bureau's speaker relations or new-talent contact. (Note: this one is for building a relationship, not for direct outreach. Commission rules differ for bureau placements.)

## How to run these

- Run one prompt per Discovery batch at 20 to 30 leads, so results stay focused and you can measure each segment's reply rate on its own.
- Start with prompts 1, 3, 6, and 9. They match what has already paid or replied, and Jeff doesn't blast them.
- Associations plan 6 to 12 months ahead. Fall 2027 conventions are being booked now. Spring 2027 is mostly set, so pitch the next-year slot like the AICPA and NAW threads did.
- The current outreach template is built for schools ("Campus Speaking Opportunity"). The trade-association rule in the Tailoring tab covers prompts 1, 2, 9, 12, and 13. Prompts 7, 8, 15, and 16 will probably need their own one-line angle before the first send.
- Think tanks are now excluded in code (October 23), and an operator focus overrides the default venue mix.
- The "Don't pull any Utah or Arizona universities" rule filters on the words "Utah" and "Arizona" anywhere in a lead's name or description, so it drops every Utah or Arizona lead, not just universities. That's why prompts 7 and 12 leave those states out.
